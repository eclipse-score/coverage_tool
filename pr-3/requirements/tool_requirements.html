score_coverage_scope shall collect, for the listed targets and their transitive in-workspace dependencies reached through deps, implementation_deps, exported_deps, components and implementation, the checked-in source and header files of cc_library and rust_library targets (srcs, hdrs) and the CrateInfo sources of rust_binary targets, and shall write them sorted and deduplicated, one workspace-relative path per line, to the allowlist file.
